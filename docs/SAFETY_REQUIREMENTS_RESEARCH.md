# Safety and compliance requirements: research for the new Safety page

Researched 2026-11-01. "Verified" means read from the primary source (Landstar's own document or the eCFR text of the regulation).
"Not verified" means it comes from memory or a secondary site and must be checked before it is relied on. Landstar can change its
standards at any time; the page keeps the effective date of each rule next to it.

## 1. Landstar (verified from Landstar's own documents)

Source: Landstar "Operator Minimum Requirements", valid as of 7/1/2026 (leasetolandstar.com, Landstar Recruiting, 800-435-4010).
Landstar adds: these are minimums, Landstar may be stricter, and qualification is in its sole discretion, case by case.

### Drivers
| Rule | Standard |
|---|---|
| Age | 23 or older |
| License | Class A CDL (Class A or B for Expedited). Non-domiciled CDLs are NOT acceptable |
| Endorsement | Hazmat endorsement (Canadian operators need a FAST card) |
| Experience | 1 year (6 months for Expedited) of verifiable over-the-road driving in the past 3 years, OR 2 years verifiable with the equipment in the last 5 years |
| Accidents | No DOT-recordable or preventable accidents in the past 12 months |
| Violations | No more than ONE serious violation in the past 36 months |
| DUI | None in the past 60 months |
| Record | Overall safe driving record |
| Felonies | No felony convictions in the past 7 years; other felony and misdemeanor charges reviewed case by case |
| Drug and alcohol | No positive drug or alcohol tests. Completing a Substance Abuse Professional (SAP) program does NOT waive this |
| English | Proficient: read traffic signs, respond to inquiries, make report entries, talk with the public |

### Equipment
- Trucks and trailers must pass a **Level One DOT inspection** at an approved Landstar inspection facility to be leased on.
- Tractor must have a **certified sleeper berth** and work with a **Landstar-approved ELD**.
- California-resident BCOs must be **CARB compliant**.
- **Landstar requires all BCO trucks and trailers to be inspected every 120 days at a Landstar-approved facility**
  (Landstar safety page). That is stricter than the federal 12-month annual inspection.
- Hot shot: one-ton dually with gooseneck, trailer 24 ft minimum, tandem 7,000 lb axles, electric breakaway brakes with battery and
  charger (Landstar FAQ dated 11/1/2022, may be out of date).
- BCOs must carry approved insurance: Unladen Liability and Occupational Accident, or statutory Workers' Compensation in some cases,
  unless they join Landstar's programs (FAQ).

### Process
- Landstar asks for a copy of the medical card and the front and back of the CDL after the application. These go through the
  **Driver Pulse app by Tenstreet**. (So Landstar uses Tenstreet.)
- Safety Thursday calls (monthly), Landstar Safety Officers (LSO) at each agency. Not requirements, but where notices come from.

### NOT found (ask Landstar or read the BCO operating agreement)
- Whether Landstar approves and keeps the file for a BCO's EMPLOYEE drivers, and what it needs for each (MVR, PSP, road test).
- Which party runs the drug and alcohol program and the random pool for those drivers.
- Which party keeps the official driver qualification file and runs the annual MVR review.
- Equipment age limits (none published). Any model-year rule heard from recruiters is unconfirmed.

## 2. FMCSA / DOT: drivers (49 CFR; verified unless marked)

| Item | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Driver qualification (DQ) file | Employment application; MVR from each state licensed in the last 3 years; road test certificate or equivalent; annual MVR; annual review note; medical certificate (CDL: CDLIS MVR with medical status); medical variance / SPE if any; National Registry verification note | 391.51(b) |
| DQ file retention | While employed + 3 years. Annual MVR, review note, medical certificate, variances may be removed 3 years after executed | 391.51(c)(d) |
| MVR at hire | Request within **30 days** of start, from every state licensed in the prior **3 years**; file within 30 days; document good-faith effort if a state sends nothing | 391.23(a)(b) |
| Previous employers | Safety performance history for the prior 3 years, within 30 days; document each contact or failed attempt | 391.23(a)(2)(c)(d) |
| Annual MVR and review | At least once every **12 months**: get the MVR (preceding 12 months), review it, file a note with the reviewer's name and date. Give great weight to speeding, reckless driving, DUI | 391.25 |
| Medical exam | Every **24 months** at most (12 months for insulin-treated diabetes, certain vision exemptions, intracity zone). Examiner must be on the National Registry | 391.45, 391.23(m) |
| CDL medical status | Verify through the CDLIS MVR. Driver self-certifies and gives the certificate to the state (383.71, not re-verified here) | 391.51, 383 |
| Clearinghouse | **Pre-employment FULL query** before any safety-sensitive work (driver consent in the Clearinghouse). **Annual query** each year (limited allowed with the driver's general consent; a hit needs a full query within 24 hours). Keep records 3 years | 382.701 |
| Pre-employment drug test | Negative result before first driving (382.301, not re-verified here) | 382 |
| Random testing | Minimum annual rates: **50% drugs, 10% alcohol** (the FMCSA can change these each January; check the year). Scientifically valid selection, unannounced, spread over the year | 382.305 |
| Other tests | Post-accident (382.303), reasonable suspicion (382.307), return-to-duty and follow-up with a SAP (Part 40). Supervisors need reasonable-suspicion training (382.603). Not re-verified here | 382 |
| Entry-level driver training (ELDT) | Required for CDLs first issued after Feb 7, 2022; check the Training Provider Registry. Not re-verified here | 380 |
| Hazmat training | At hire (within 90 days) and every 3 years; keep records. Not re-verified here | 49 CFR 172.704 |
| Hours of service | 11-hour driving, 14-hour window, 30-minute break after 8 hours driving, 60/70 hours in 7/8 days, 34-hour restart; ELD; carrier keeps records of duty status 6 months. Not re-verified here | Part 395 |
| English proficiency | A driver must read and speak English well enough to do the job. Not re-verified here | 391.11(b)(2) |
| PSP (Pre-Employment Screening Program) | Optional report: about 5 years of crashes and 3 years of roadside inspections. Needs the driver's consent; about **$10** per report (not verified; check psp.fmcsa.dot.gov). Cannot be pulled on a current employee | FMCSA PSP |
| Roadside inspection reports | Driver gives the carrier the report; keep 12 months. Not re-verified here | 396.9 |
| Accident register | Record every DOT-recordable crash (fatal, injury treated away from the scene, tow-away); keep 3 years. Not re-verified here | 390.15 |

## 3. FMCSA / DOT: vehicles (verified unless marked)

| Item | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Annual (periodic) inspection | Every tractor, trailer: passed inspection **within the last 12 months**; documentation (report or sticker) on the vehicle with date, who keeps the report, vehicle ID, certification passed. Inspector must be qualified (396.19). Government (state) program inspections count | 396.17 |
| Driver vehicle inspection report (DVIR) | Written at the end of each day for each vehicle, driver signs; defects must be repaired and certified before the vehicle goes out again; keep **3 months**. No report needed if no defects. Electronic allowed | 396.11 |
| Landstar interval | Every **120 days** at a Landstar-approved facility (stricter than the 12 months above) | Landstar |
| Maintenance records | Keep records for each vehicle (inspection, repair, maintenance), at the carrier, 1 year while in service + 6 months after. Not re-verified here | 396.3 |
| Brakes, lights, tires, etc. | Part 393 parts and accessories; the inspection checklist is Appendix A to Part 396 | 393, 396 |
| Registration, IRP, IFTA, UCR, insurance | Mostly held by the carrier of record (Landstar) under the lease. Track expiries if JeanWay holds any | 387, 390 |

## 4. How to get a Motor Vehicle Record (MVR)

An employer may get a driver's record with the driver's written consent (and a permitted use under the federal Driver's Privacy
Protection Act). A record bought through a screening company is also a "consumer report" under the Fair Credit Reporting Act, which
needs a stand-alone disclosure, written authorization, and a pre-adverse and adverse action notice before refusing someone for it.

| Option | How it works | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Washington DOL, direct | Business account on **License eXpress**; keep the driver's signed Driving Record Release of Interest in your files; the record is available for 30 days | **$15** per record (DOL form; confirm on dol.wa.gov) | Washington licence only. Other states each have their own process. Cheapest, but one state at a time |
| Screening company with an API | HireRight (all 50 states + DC), Tenstreet (Landstar already uses it), DISA, Checkr, SambaSafety and others. Order in their site; some have an API so FleetFlow could order and receive the record | Roughly state fee + vendor fee, often $20 to $40 each (not verified, quote needed) | Handles every state, consent forms, continuous monitoring, FCRA notices. Needs a contract |
| Continuous monitoring | The vendor tells you when a driver gets a new violation or the licence changes | Per driver per month or year, quote needed | FMCSA guidance says a complete, automatically updated record, reviewed by a manager with a log, can serve the annual review; some compliance firms advise still documenting an annual review |
| Landstar | Landstar vets BCO drivers through its own process (Tenstreet). It may already pull and review MVRs. Ask whether you can receive a copy | Included? | Must be confirmed with Landstar |

The record shows licence status, points and violations by date. Landstar's rules read straight off it: a serious violation in the
last 36 months (more than one fails), a DUI in the last 60 months, a DOT-recordable or preventable accident in the last 12 months.
